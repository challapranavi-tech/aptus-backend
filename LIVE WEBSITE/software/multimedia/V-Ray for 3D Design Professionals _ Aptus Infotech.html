Unleash Photorealistic Power in SketchUp

In the world of 3D design and architectural visualization, the standard for presenting a vision is photorealism. Chaos V-Ray for SketchUp is the industry-standard rendering plugin, transforming your robust SketchUp models into stunning, lifelike images, animations, and immersive experiences with production-ready quality.

By working within V-Ray’s advanced rendering environment, you leverage the power of physically-based light and materials to accurately simulate real-world conditions. V-Ray’s Interactive Rendering and V-Ray Vision (real-time) modes allow you to make critical design changes—adjusting a camera, tweaking a texture, or repositioning a light—and see the final, high-fidelity result instantaneously, accelerating your creative flow.

From rapid design iteration and creating precise material studies to generating final, award-winning stills and animations, V-Ray provides the comprehensive, professional toolset that ensures your SketchUp designs are visualized with ultimate realism and delivered with efficiency.

Powerful Rendering Features

Experience the industry-standard rendering engine optimized for SketchUp

Global Illumination

Realistic light simulation with advanced GI algorithms that accurately calculate indirect light bouncing throughout your scene for natural-looking results.

Physically-Based Materials

Create stunning materials using V-Ray’s comprehensive material system with accurate reflection, refraction, and subsurface scattering properties.

Interactive Rendering

Experience real-time feedback with V-Ray’s progressive rendering mode, allowing you to see changes instantly as you adjust lights and materials.

Advanced Camera Controls

Precise camera control with depth of field, motion blur, and exposure settings to create professional photography-quality renders.

Cloud Collaboration

Render remotely using V-Ray Cloud or share scenes with colleagues through Trimble Connect for seamless collaboration.

Extension Support

Enhance your workflow with V-Ray extensions including VR panorama creation, batch rendering, and custom shader development tools.
