Transform Your Designs into Stunning Visuals

Lumion is powerful 3D rendering software that makes it easy to transform your CAD models into beautiful images, videos, and 360 panoramas. With its intuitive interface and real-time rendering capabilities, Lumion helps architects and designers communicate their vision with breathtaking visuals.

Designed for professionals who need to create high-quality visualizations quickly, Lumion seamlessly integrates with your favorite modeling software. Its vast content library and powerful effects allow you to add life, context, and emotion to your designs with just a few clicks.

Whether you’re creating architectural visualizations, urban planning projects, or landscape designs, Lumion provides the tools you need to impress clients and stakeholders with photorealistic results.

Powerful Features

Discover why architects worldwide choose Lumion

Real-Time Rendering

Experience the speed of real-time rendering as you make changes to your scene. See updates instantly and iterate quickly to perfect your visualization.

Stunning Visual Effects

Create photorealistic images and videos with advanced effects including volumetric lighting, atmospheric rain, and customizable materials.

Extensive Content Library

Access thousands of high-quality models, materials, and objects to bring your scenes to life. From furniture to vegetation, find everything you need.

Seamless Integration

Work with your favorite modeling software including Revit, SketchUp, ArchiCAD, Rhino, and more. Import models with LiveSync for real-time synchronization.

360° Panoramas

Create immersive 360° panoramas that allow clients to experience your designs from every angle. Perfect for presentations and virtual walkthroughs.

Video Creation

Produce professional-quality videos with cinematic camera effects, smooth transitions, and customizable paths to tell the story of your design.
