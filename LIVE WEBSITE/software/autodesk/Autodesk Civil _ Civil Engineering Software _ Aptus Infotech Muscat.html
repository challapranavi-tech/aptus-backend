Design Smarter, Faster, and More Accurately

In today’s complex construction and land development sector, precision, efficiency, and collaboration are critical.

Autodesk Civil 3D Solutions help engineering firms and government agencies enhance project accuracy and accelerate design timelines.

Whether designing a simple road or modeling a complex highway interchange with stormwater management, this advanced software improves the way civil engineering projects are planned, analyzed, and documented.

With powerful tools for corridor modeling, grading optimization, and geospatial analysis, Civil 3D ensures seamless data management and superior modeling efficiency across all land development and infrastructure projects.

Features such as dynamic 3D surfaces, intelligent grading, automated plan production, and integrated GIS capabilities provide end-to-end solutions that deliver accurate, compliant, and professional civil designs every time.

Powerful Features

Discover why Civil 3D is the preferred choice for civil engineering professionals worldwide

Corridor Modeling

Create complex 3D models of transportation corridors with dynamic alignment, profile, and assembly tools that automatically update when design changes occur.

Parcel & Grading

Design land parcels with flexible layout tools and create detailed grading plans with automated surface generation and volume calculations.

Hydrology & Hydraulics

Analyze stormwater management, design drainage systems, and create detailed pipe networks with specialized hydrology and hydraulic analysis tools.

Survey & Data Processing

Import and process survey data from multiple sources, create surfaces, and generate accurate topographic maps and contour plans.

Quantity Takeoff

Automatically calculate material quantities and generate detailed reports directly from your 3D model, improving estimation accuracy.

Automated Documentation

Generate construction drawings, profiles, cross-sections, and annotation that automatically update when design changes are made.
