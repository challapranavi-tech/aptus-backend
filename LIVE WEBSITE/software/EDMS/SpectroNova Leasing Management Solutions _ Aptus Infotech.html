Transform Project Management with SpectroNova

SpectroNova, powered by Aptus Infotech, is an advanced and integrated Project Management Information System (PMIS) meticulously designed to empower organizations in effectively managing, tracking, and controlling complex projects, programs, and portfolios. Building on over 25 years of industry experience, SpectroNova provides a robust, centralized platform that offers real-time visibility and control across every phase of a project lifecycle, from initial capital planning and project delivery to cost control and facilities management.
Request a Quote

Key Features of SpectroNova

Comprehensive tools designed specifically for end-to-end project lifecycle management

Advanced Planning & Scheduling

Develop comprehensive project schedules with a built-in powerful scheduling tool that integrates with industry standards like Primavera P6 and MS Project. Track tasks, milestones, and dependencies with precision.

Resource & Financial Management

Efficiently manage project resources, control costs, track earned value, and gain deep financial insights to ensure projects remain on budget and optimize profitability.

Issue, Risk & Change Management

Proactively identify, track, and mitigate project risks. Manage changes effectively through customizable processes, ensuring smooth execution and minimizing disruptions.

Tendering, Bid & Subcontract Management

Streamline procurement processes, manage bids, pre-qualify suppliers, and oversee subcontractor performance and payments within the system.

Centralized Document Hub

Act as a single source of truth for all project documentation, including 2D/3D designs, drawings, contracts, and correspondence. Store, search, retrieve, and archive unlimited sizes and formats of documents with high security and 99% uptime.

Intelligent Workflow Automation (BPM)

Implement built-in Business Process Management (BPM) tools to define, automate, and re-engineer workflows. This enables digital collaboration for stakeholder approvals, RFI routing, transmittals, and other construction documents.

Benefits for Your Organization

How SpectroNova transforms project delivery and organizational efficiency

Reduced Project Delays

Improve communication and streamline workflows across all stakeholders to minimize costly delays and disruptions.

Significant Cost Savings
