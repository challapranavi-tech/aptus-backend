Transform Construction Project Management with Oracle Aconex

Oracle Aconex is a powerful cloud-based construction project management platform that streamlines communication, documentation, and workflow coordination across the entire project lifecycle. Designed to improve team collaboration and centralize all project data in a secure common data environment (CDE), it enhances efficiency for construction, engineering, and infrastructure projects. 

With strong features for document control, quality tracking, risk management, and digital collaboration, Oracle Aconex supports smoother project delivery, helps reduce delays, improves compliance, and ensures projects are completed safely, on time, and within budget while aligning with local construction industry standards and best practices.

Key Features of Oracle Aconex

Comprehensive tools designed specifically for the construction industry

Document Control & Collaboration

Centralized document management with version control, markup tools, and real-time collaboration for all project stakeholders.

Risk Management & Safety

Integrated risk register, hazard identification, inspection tracking, and safety management tools to ensure compliance and worker safety.

Quality Assurance & Punch Lists

Defect tracking, non-conformance reports, and punch list management with automated workflows and resolution tracking.

Project Scheduling & Progress Tracking

Gantt chart visualization, progress monitoring, and schedule analysis tools with real-time updates and alerts.

Communication Hub

Integrated messaging, meeting minutes, and action item tracking to keep all stakeholders aligned and informed.

Mobile Accessibility

Native mobile apps for iOS and Android with offline capability, allowing field teams to work efficiently anywhere.
