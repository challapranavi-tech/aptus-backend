Digital Twin in the AEC Industry
Digital Twin technology is reshaping the Architecture, Engineering, and Construction
industry by turning buildings and infrastructure into intelligent, data-driven systems. At
its core, a digital twin is a dynamic virtual replica of a physical asset. It’s built using real-
time data from sensors, IoT devices, BIM models, and other digital inputs. But it’s more
than just a 3D model—it’s a living, learning system that reflects the real-world
performance and condition of structures across their lifecycle.
Real-time Data Connection: Sensors on the physical object (like a
turbine, vehicle, or even a city) collect data and continuously send it
to the digital twin.
Analysis and Simulation: This virtual model can be used to
analyze performance, run simulations, predict potential faults,
and test changes before they are implemented in the physical
world.
Virtual Model: The digital twin is a computer-generated
model that uses this data to accurately replicate the
physical object's state and behavior.
Two-Way Flow: The data flow is often two-way,
meaning insights from the digital twin can inform
decisions and actions taken in the real world.

Solar Irrigation
Survive the dry season in Oman with Solar Powered Irrigation
Systems (SPIS). With high temperatures, the need for irrigating
farmlands increases as well. With solar powered water pumps,
farmers can irrigate their crops in the right amount and at the
right time. The change to solar power means you can lower your
operating costs.
Solar PV Module
Solar Photovoltaic module is created by connecting several PV
cells. The module generates electric energy by converting light
energy captured by the PV cells. PV modules come with an
inverter that converts DC current into AC current. This energy is
used for lighting residential and commercial buildings and the
excess energy is sold to the grid (in case of on-grid PV systems).
